# Pharmacy Performance Dashboard
## Pharmacy Sales Analysis Report

### 1. Objective
To analyze pharmacy sales, quantity sold, product mix,
and business performance using the supplied dataset.

### 2. Dataset Overview
The dataset contains 10 patient/product records with
fields including diagnosis, primary medication, dosage,
treatment duration, readmission, blood pressure, and
cholesterol level.

Important limitation:
The supplied dataset appears to contain clinical and
treatment information rather than pharmacy sales data.
Therefore, sales revenue, product cost, profit margin,
and branded-versus-generic sales cannot be calculated
reliably from this dataset.

### 3. Data Validation
The following fields were reviewed:
- Patient ID
- Age and gender
- Diagnosis
- Primary medication
- Dosage
- Treatment duration
- Readmission status
- Blood pressure
- Cholesterol level

Validation checks:
- Confirm that patient IDs are unique.
- Check that age and dosage values are valid.
- Standardize medication names and units.
- Check missing or inconsistent values.
- Verify that readmission values use a consistent format.

### 4. KPI Assessment

| KPI | Status |
|---|---|
| Total sales revenue | Not calculable |
| Total units sold | Not calculable |
| Product cost | Not available |
| Gross profit | Not calculable |
| Profit margin | Not calculable |
| Branded vs generic sales | Not calculable |
| Readmission count | Requires clinical data validation |

### 5. Branded vs Generic Comparison
A reliable comparison requires a product sales dataset
containing product name, brand/generic classification,
quantity sold, selling price, and product cost.

These fields are not available in the supplied dataset.
No unsupported sales figures have been assumed.

### 6. Recommendations
1. Obtain the pharmacy sales transaction dataset.
2. Include product name and product category.
3. Record units sold and selling price.
4. Add product cost to calculate gross margin.
5. Validate dates, quantities, and missing values.
6. Build a dashboard showing revenue, units sold,
   product contribution, and branded/generic performance.

### 7. Limitations
This dataset primarily contains clinical information.
It does not provide the sales, quantity, cost, category,
or transaction-date fields required for the requested
pharmacy performance dashboard.

Clinical data should not be used to infer sales or profit.

### 8. Conclusion
The available data can support a preliminary review of
patient and treatment fields, subject to validation.
A pharmacy sales dataset is required to calculate
accurate business KPIs and create the requested
performance dashboard.
