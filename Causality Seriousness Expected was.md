# Task 03: Causality, Seriousness, and Expectedness

## 1. Objective
To assess four fictional Velunex safety cases using causality assessment, seriousness classification, and expectedness assessment.

This report is for training purposes only.

## 2. Expectedness Reference

The fictional Velunex reference lists the following expected adverse reactions:
- Transient headache
- Nausea
- Dizziness
- Mild, self-limiting rash

Any event not listed in this reference is classified as unexpected for this exercise.

## 3. Case Assessments

### Case PV-201: Dizziness

**Case Summary:**
Dizziness began two hours after the first use of Velunex and resolved the same day. No treatment or hospitalisation was required.

**Causality Assessment (WHO-UMC):**
Possible. The event occurred shortly after product use, supporting a possible relationship. However, further clinical information is unavailable to establish a stronger causal relationship.

**Seriousness Classification:**
Non-serious. No death, life-threatening event, hospitalisation, disability, or other serious outcome was reported.

**Expectedness:**
Expected. Dizziness is listed in the supplied Velunex reference.

**Reporting Implication:**
Document the case and report it according to the applicable training or organisational procedures.

**Conclusion:**
A non-serious, expected event with a possible relationship to Velunex.

---

### Case PV-202: Appendicitis

**Case Summary:**
Appendicitis occurred three weeks after first use and required hospitalisation. The medical history is incomplete.

**Causality Assessment (WHO-UMC):**
Unassessable/Unclassifiable. The available information is insufficient to evaluate the relationship reliably. The time interval and incomplete medical history prevent a firm conclusion.

**Seriousness Classification:**
Serious. Hospitalisation is a seriousness criterion.

**Expectedness:**
Unexpected. Appendicitis is not listed in the supplied Velunex reference.

**Reporting Implication:**
Document the serious, unexpected event and seek additional medical history and relevant clinical information. Follow applicable expedited reporting procedures where required.

**Conclusion:**
A serious, unexpected event with an unassessable causal relationship based on the information available.

---

### Case PV-203: Mild Rash

**Case Summary:**
A mild rash began on day four and improved after stopping Velunex. No rechallenge was performed.

**Causality Assessment (WHO-UMC):**
Possible. The timing and improvement after stopping the product support a possible relationship. However, alternative causes cannot be excluded, and there was no rechallenge.

**Seriousness Classification:**
Non-serious. The rash was described as mild, and no serious outcome was reported.

**Expectedness:**
Expected. A mild, self-limiting rash is listed in the supplied Velunex reference.

**Reporting Implication:**
Document the event and follow applicable organisational reporting procedures.

**Conclusion:**
A non-serious, expected event with a possible relationship to Velunex.
